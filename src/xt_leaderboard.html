What one square means
The model's estimated chance that a possession ends in a goal when a completed pass ends in that location. It is not the chance of scoring from a shot there.
How to read the colors
Darker green means a lower estimate; orange and yellow mean higher. The colors stretch the middle 90% of estimates to make differences visible; hover values are the actual model estimates.
What xT added means
xT added = estimate at end − estimate at start
For example, moving from a 2% zone to a 4% zone adds about 2 percentage points of estimated scoring chance. Player totals sum these action-by-action changes.
This map holds the action context constant (completed, unpressured pass) and varies only its end location. It explains the location signal used by the rankings; each action's xT also depends on its start location and context.

Methodology. Expected Threat scored by a LightGBM classifier trained on 5-fold match-level cross-validation (CV AUC 0.602 ± 0.035) — see project README for the deep learning comparison and multi-task xG model. Minutes played computed from Starting XI and substitution events; red-card exits aren't modeled, so a small number of players may show slightly inflated minutes. Data: StatsBomb Open Data, FIFA World Cup 2022.
